A result is stored by the backend, in memory and above a limit on disk. Sorting, filtering, searching, adding up and comparing happen there, so the window never holds more than it paints.

JOIN completion

It knows your schema. Even the join.

Tables behind FROM, the columns of an alias behind o., and behind JOIN the whole condition, taken from the foreign key.

An error is underlined at the place the database names, with its code, detail and hint beside it.

WHERE tables as tables

Filter, sort, follow the key.

Open a table and filter it with conditions; the values are always bound, never written into the SQL. Sorting happens on the server.

F4 on customer_id = 3 shows that customer, Alt+F7 lists every table that points to the row, with counts.

UPDATE edit data

Change cells. Nothing is sent while you type.

Changes are collected and shown in colour: changed, new, to delete. Look at them as SQL, undo them, then write them together in one transaction: all rows or none.

A row somebody else changed in the meantime is reported, not overwritten.

F2 Cmd/Ctrl+S Cmd/Ctrl+D

EXPLAIN execution plan

See where the time goes.

The plan of every database as one tree table: rows estimated and actual, own and total time. The expensive operation and the wrong estimate stand out, with a hint in words.

“With execution” runs the statement inside a transaction that is rolled back.

WHERE customer_id = :customer AND id IN (:ids)

Parameters

:name, ${name}, ? or $1: the app asks for the values when the statement runs, remembers them per tab and hands them to the driver. A value never becomes part of the SQL text, the history or a log.

CONNECT seven drivers, eleven databases

Your databases. Behind a tunnel, if they are.

Profiles in folders with colour tags, passwords only in the keychain of your system. A connection comes from a URL, or from what DBeaver, DataGrip, .pgpass or .my.cnf already know.

GRANT read, to an assistant

Let your AI ask. You decide what runs.

Over the Model Context Protocol, an assistant on the same computer reads the structure, runs reading queries within limits and proposes statements. Anything that writes waits for your click on a statement you can read.

Off by default. No port while it is off. Each connection gets its own level.
